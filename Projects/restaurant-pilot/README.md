# Restaurant pilot

**Status from the supplied plan:** owner contacted; one topic and usable data still pending.

Select one decision: product pricing, staff wages, staffing levels, opening hours or days open/closed. Do not promise answers to all five.

## PIL1 — Scope and data check

Record the question, decision owner, relevant period/grain, required fields/costs, constraints, permissions and source control totals. Inspect a small export before promising the result.

If the evidence cannot support the question, narrow it or propose data collection.

## PIL2 — Bounded free delivery

After the relevant skill checks, aim for one question and an agreed dataset, starting with 8–12 weeks if available and comparable. The supplied plan proposes about 12–18 client hours after scoping.

Deliver reproducible calculations, a QA log, one-page findings, two or three charts, a discussion and one revision.

Revenue alone does not establish profit, a good wage, overstaffing or the best opening schedule. State missing costs, seasonality, service constraints and uncertainty.

## PIL3 — Feedback and next step

Record what the owner actually understood, used or rejected. Separate feedback, a testimonial, a paid offer and a purchase.

A paid follow-up can be discussed, but delivery follows the Phase 2 + Agent/API entry milestone and engagement-specific checks. Study continues if the owner is delayed.
